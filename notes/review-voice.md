# Mariposa — Review Voice (copy source of truth)

Compiled from TripAdvisor (4.9★ · 288 reviews · Travelers' Choice 2025), Google
reviews, the WordPress export, and the six attributed excerpts already in
`brand.ts`. This is the well every new line of copy draws from. The awe on the
page should feel **earned** because it echoes what hundreds of guests already
said. Quote reviews only in the Reviews section (attributed, linked). Everywhere
else, transmute these themes into original brand language.

Governed by `stop-slop`. Banned: nestled, boasts, culinary journey, elevate,
tapestry, symphony, hidden away, in the heart of, tantalize, exclamation marks,
"not just X but Y", tricolon crutches, em/en dashes in visible copy. Positive
assertive constructions only. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. Scenes over
adjectives.

---

## 1. The emotional language guests actually use

Recurring, in rough order of frequency:

- **gem** — "a true gem", "a true gem in Rhodes" (the single most repeated word;
  earns its place in the hero)
- **family / family-run** — "Constantine and his family make the experience
  special with their warmth"
- **home / made with love** — the kitchen reads as someone's home, not a service
- **warm / warmth** — of the welcome, the host, the room
- **garden / calm** — the terrace under the vines, unhurried
- **best on the island / best in Rhodes** — "by far the best restaurant we've
  tried on the island"
- **unforgettable / special / first class / exceptional**
- **discovery** — "what a fabulous discovery"

Tone to write toward: quiet certainty, warmth without sentimentality, the
confidence of a room that already knows it is loved. Never hard-sell.

## 2. Specific scenes guests describe (use these as concrete detail)

- **The chef picking herbs at arrival:** "the owner's wife and also chef was
  picking the lemon herbs to make more when we arrived." → Despoina in the
  garden. The freshest possible signal: the dish begins after you sit down.
- **The vine canopy:** "outdoor tables under a cover of vine-type plants which
  give plenty of shade." → the setting; dappled light, leaf shadow.
- **The farewell liqueur:** complimentary brandy / local liqueur to close the
  night. → Konstantin's hospitality, the night ending generously.
- **Leftovers boxed with care:** "lovely little cardboard boxes with lids and a
  paper bag to take our leftovers." → nothing is grudged; you leave looked after.
- **The owner at every table:** "from the owner greeting us throughout the
  evening." → Konstantin receives and tends every table, arrival to last sip.
- **Proposals under the vines** (brief): many have happened here, and they tend
  to succeed. → Celebrations section.

## 4. The family — narrative spine (names/roles exact, per brief)

> Despoina built it. Konstantin opens the door. Mara carries the warmth.
> Nickolas mixes the night. Salvatore writes the next chapter.

- **Despoina** — mother. Origin of the menu, the dishes, and the decor. The
  cuisine and the aesthetic both come from her hands. The inspiration the whole
  concept flows from.
- **Konstantin** — father (the export's "Ntinos/Konstantinos Diakosavvas", the
  "owner greeting us throughout the evening"). The welcome. Receives every guest,
  tends the table from arrival to the last sip.
- **Mara** — daughter. The heart of the hospitality and the service.
- **Nickolas** — son. Programmer by day, master of the cocktail bar by night.
- **Salvatore** — future son-in-law, from Sicily. Years as a chef; fresh pasta
  and homemade bread are his craft. Carries Despoina's kitchen tradition
  forward. The rising star of the house.

Two cuisines in one kitchen — Despoina's Greek garden table and Salvatore's
Sicilian hand with pasta and bread — is a genuinely rare story. Let it surface
in Chef's Words.

## 5. The three differentiators (Garden & Philosophy)

1. **The garden** — vegetables grown steps from the table, picked the morning
   they are served.
2. **The small, changing menu** — few dishes, chosen and replaced often, every
   plate cooked fresh; flavors you rarely meet twice.
3. **The setting** — tables among the plants, an earthy calm under the vines.

## 6. House principle (the brand's true center)

Whoever walks in is received as a guest of the family. Express through scene,
never as a slogan or a "not X but Y" line. Section pull-quote, phrased
positively: **"Every table here is set the way we set our own."**

## 7. Concrete details to reach for (awe through specificity)

- the lemon herbs picked at your arrival
- the bread from Salvatore's oven that morning
- the liqueur that ends the night
- the leftovers boxed with care
- the vine shade, the dawn-picked vegetables, the handful of covers each service
